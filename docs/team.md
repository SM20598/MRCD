# About the authors
The authors of this work are affiliated with the [Institute of Logistics Engineering](https//.tuhh.itl.de) at [Hamburg University of Technology](https://www.tuhh.de/tuhh/startseite). Our primary research focus is robotics and their application to the logistics context. MRCD is part of [TaBuLa-LOGplus](https://www3.tuhh.de/itl/en/tabula-logplus-english/), a project on automated public transport services and robots.

---

If you are interested in working with us, please reachout to the institute or us directly.
