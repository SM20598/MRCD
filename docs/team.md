# About the authors
The authors of this work are affiliated with the Institute of Logistics Engineering (ITL) at Hamburg University of Technology. Our primary research focus is robotics and their application to the logistics context. For more information on the Institute and other projects, please visit the hompage of [ITL](https//.tuhh.itl.de).

Below, you find detailed information on the main authors of MRCD. If you are interested in working with us, please feel free to reach out :)
